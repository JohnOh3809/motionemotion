Emotion, read from how you move

Not everyone shows what they feel on their face. This watches your body instead — posture, energy, how sharply you move — and tells you what it reads.

  • Nothing leaves your device. The camera feed is processed in your browser and never uploaded.
  • It admits when it's unsure instead of guessing — an app shouldn't speak over you.
  • It calibrates to you. Three seconds of standing still teaches it your normal.

Your browser will ask permission. Stand back so your upper body fits in frame.
